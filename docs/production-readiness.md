# Production-readiness gates

Swarm runs, demonstrates and has been tested as an application. It is **not production ready**, and
no formal review has been completed. This page lists the gates a service of this kind would be held
to, and says honestly which are met.

How to read it:

- **PASS** needs executable evidence that anyone can re-run, named in the row.
- **PARTIAL** means part of the gate is met; the row says which part.
- **NOT MET** and **NOT STARTED** mean exactly that.
- **READY FOR REVIEW**, **BLOCKED ON OWNER**, **NOT STARTED** and **NOT APPLICABLE** are the only statuses a
  governance gate can have here. Such a gate needs a person with the standing to sign it off, so it is never
  marked PASS: the sign-off is not the author's to give. READY FOR REVIEW means the material for the reviewer
  exists and nobody has reviewed it; BLOCKED ON OWNER means a decision that only the owner can make is
  missing. The [review checklist](./pitch/human-review-checklist.md) says what to put in front of each reviewer.

## Reading the table

Of the 29 gates above, 8 are met in the narrow sense stated, 11 are partly met, 5 are not met, and 5 are
governance gates that a person must pass: 3 ready for review, 1 blocked on its owner and 1 not started. What stands between this and a hosted pilot, as opposed to a
demonstration, is mostly things that exist in the repository but have never met the real thing: sign-in
against a real Entra tenant (it needs an app registration that only a tenant administrator can create), the
PostgreSQL adapter and the job queue against a deployed server and separate containers, telemetry sent to a real
Application Insights resource, and the hosting deployment itself, which awaits approval for chargeable
resources. A decision on audit-log retention is also open. They are the first items in the
[roadmap](./pitch/roadmap.md).

# Human review checklist

The reviews that only a person with the standing to give them can pass. **Every row is NOT SIGNED
OFF.** Nothing in this repository marks a review as passed, and the author's own self-review is not a
substitute for any of them. For each review: what to put in front of the reviewer, what the author has
already done, and the questions most worth asking.

# Risk register

Risks to a pilot of Swarm, as understood at the end of this program. "In place" lists controls that
exist and are tested; "Remains" is what they do not cover. Likelihood and impact are the author's
judgement (H, M, L), not a measurement. A person who owns the risk should re-rate them.

Status of the product these risks apply to: an application that runs locally and has been
demonstrated against a real Azure model. It is not hosted and not a production service.
See [the production-readiness gates](../production-readiness.md).

## Evidence and AI behaviour

| ID  | Risk                                                                                | L   | I   | In place                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Remains                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- | --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R1  | A statement that no source supports reaches a decision maker looking like a finding | M   | H   | Type and support class on every statement; a deterministic publication policy; unsupported statements are labelled hypotheses ([ADR 0001](../adr/0001-evidence-integrity-model.md))                                         | The deterministic check accepted 3 of 8 unsupported statements in the labelled set. The model check lowers a verdict but is measured only in single live runs. Reviewers are not required to act                                                                        |
| R2  | The analyst answers from the model's general knowledge when the sources are silent  | M   | H   | A deterministic gate refuses without a model call; answers must cite or abstain ([ADR 0002](../adr/0002-abstention-and-citation-rule.md)); uncited answers are labelled                                                     | The gate refuses 43% (dev) / 86% (test) of unanswerable questions, and 1 of 4 plausible-absent ones. A mixed question ("weather next Tuesday") passes it. Only a live model run shows the rest                                                                          |
| R3  | A conflict between sources goes unseen, or a false alarm wastes reviewer time       | H   | M   | Candidates are shown until a person confirms or dismisses; the demos show a miss and two false alarms openly; only sentences a passage holds whole are compared, and a sentence in the overlap between passages counts once | 1 of 3 labelled conflicts found (precision 100% on the labelled set); semantic and anaphoric conflicts are missed; a sentence longer than the 120-character overlap that straddles a cut is held whole by no passage and is not compared. No model-assisted pass exists |
| R4  | Instructions hidden in a source change what the model does (prompt injection)       | M   | H   | Sources are passed as data; models have no tools; outputs are schema-checked; echoing an embedded payload is rejected; patterns warn                                                                                        | Warnings caught 80% of labelled samples (in-sample) and miss obfuscated and non-English text. One live probe resisted it. This cannot be called solved                                                                                                                  |
| R5  | Behaviour changes when the model or deployment changes                              | M   | M   | `npm run azure:verify` runs 15 checks against a live deployment                                                                                                                                                             | The evidence is one live run per milestone. There is no regression suite that calls a model                                                                                                                                                                             |
| R6  | Simulated personas are read as predictions, or encode stereotypes                   | M   | H   | Personas are labelled fictional proxies and assumed input; the product says it explores scenarios and does not predict                                                                                                      | No Responsible-AI review, no bias or fairness testing, no content-safety layer beyond the provider's filter                                                                                                                                                             |
| R7  | Evaluation results do not transfer to real documents                                | H   | M   | Results are published with their limits ([evaluation README](../../evaluation/README.md))                                                                                                                                   | Sets are small, in one fictional domain, and labelled by the author. A pilot needs a labelled set from real documents                                                                                                                                                   |

## Identity, data and operations

| ID  | Risk                                                                       | L   | I   | In place                                                                                                                                                               | Remains                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- | --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R8  | Sign-in is wrong in a way a local test provider cannot show                | M   | H   | Token checks are tested against sixteen kinds of bad token; four mutations were caught ([ADR 0005](../adr/0005-identity-roles-and-tenancy.md))                         | No real tenant has been used. The browser has no sign-in. An app registration and app roles are needed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| R9  | One tenant sees another's data                                             | L   | H   | Tenant and owner filters on every lookup; the same 404 as a missing project; tests for lists, counts, audit, branches and a comparison that names another tenant's run | Filtering is in application code only. No row-level security. One owner per project; no sharing model. The guard covers only the path identifier: a route given a second experiment identifier must check it itself. One was missed and found by an independent review, now fixed; a tripwire test fails the build when a route gains a new identifier field, but it cannot tell whether the handler checks it |
| R10 | The database cannot serve more than one instance                           | H   | M   | SQLite with versioned migrations and a backup before upgrade                                                                                                           | Hosted multi-user use needs PostgreSQL and an asynchronous repository ([ADR 0006](../adr/0006-postgres-adapter-proposed.md)). | R11 | The audit trail is rewritten, or keeps personal data that should be erased | L   | M   | Append-only triggers and a hash chain; entries hold ids and counts, never content                                                                                      | The head of the chain is not anchored outside the database. | R12 | Token spend runs away, or one tenant starves the others                    | M   | M   | A call budget per operation; a 15-minute budget per run; a rate limiter; a project limit per tenant; token counts recorded per project                                 | No per-tenant token budget or quota; no cost alert. The [cost model](../pitch/cost-model.md) is an estimate                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| R13 | A run is lost, or hangs, with no one noticing                              | M   | M   | Pause and resume from saved rounds; an interrupted run is marked failed, with a retry that starts a new attempt                                                        | Nothing resumes a run automatically after a restart. No distributed tracing or alerting; logs only                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| R14 | A vulnerable or tampered dependency                                        | L   | H   | Few dependencies; lockfile with integrity hashes; `npm audit` (0 vulnerabilities at last run); history and working-tree secret scan in CI                              | No software bill of materials or signed provenance. The `jose` entry in the lockfile was added through a package mirror and is verified only when CI runs `npm ci`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| R15 | The AGPL-3.0 licence obliges the operator of a hosted copy                 | M   | M   | The licence is declared; no code was taken from the reference project (see [the parity matrix](../parity-matrix.md))                                                   | Legal review of the network-use clause before any hosted pilot, and of any fork of this work                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| R16 | The interface is unusable for some people                                  | M   | M   | Automated axe checks at four viewports on the main journey and the evidence ledger; keyboard tests; reduced-motion support                                             | Dark and high-contrast themes are derived, not designed; the forced-colours rules have no browser test; no screen-reader or assisted-technology testing; a formal accessibility review has not been done                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

## Not risks of the product, but open

- The persistent Azure development machine requested earlier is **not provisioned**. It needs your
  approval for chargeable resources, and none was given. The prepared work is on a local branch.
- Azure hosting of Swarm itself is **not started**. The only Azure resources in use are the existing
  development AI resources, which were not changed.

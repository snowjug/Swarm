# Northgate Bus Company: submission on the redesign

This is a fictional submission written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## Usage

Bus ridership in 2025 was 7.4 million boardings, from the company's ticketing records.

## Cost

The company estimates that the redesign would cost $61 million over five years, because driver recruitment and new depot space are not in the Authority's figure. It supports higher frequency on the main corridors but warns that withdrawing routes will isolate some villages.

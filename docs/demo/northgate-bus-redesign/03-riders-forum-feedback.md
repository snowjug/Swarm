# Northgate Riders' Forum: feedback on the redesign

This is a fictional feedback note written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## What riders told the Forum

Riders on the withdrawn routes say they have no other way to reach the hospital or the market town. Commuters on the main corridors welcome a bus every 10 minutes. Many riders ask what will replace the withdrawn routes for people who cannot walk to a main corridor.

## What the Forum asks

The Forum asks for a demand-responsive service for the villages that would lose their route, and for the consultation to run for at least six months.

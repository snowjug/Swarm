# Larkholm Residents' Association: ferry survey summary

This is a fictional survey summary written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## Who answered

The Association surveyed 1,150 islander households and received replies from 52% of them.

## What islanders said

68% of respondents ranked year-round reliability above emissions reductions. 71% oppose a reduced winter timetable, and 64% oppose a 12% fare increase. 52% said they would support an electric ferry if six sailings a day were kept in winter.

## Who depends on the ferry in winter

The Larkholm Health Centre reports about 140 patient trips a month to mainland clinics between November and March, including dialysis appointments that cannot be rescheduled. Many nurses, teachers and shift workers commute by the first and last sailings of the day.

## What the Association asks

The Association asks the Council to guarantee at least six winter sailings a day and to cap any fare increase at 5%. It will not oppose an electric ferry that meets these two conditions.

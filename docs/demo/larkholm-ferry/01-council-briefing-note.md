# Larkholm Island Council: ferry renewal briefing note

This is a fictional briefing note written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## Background

The island's only ferry, the diesel vessel MV Gannet, is 27 years old. Its maintenance bill has risen every year since 2021, and the operating contract with the Larkholm Ferry Company ends in 2028.

## Usage

Council counts show 412,000 one-way crossings in 2025. Of these, 61% took place between June and September.

## Options before the Council

Option 1 is a diesel refit costing $3.1 million, which would extend the vessel's life by about eight years. Option 2 is a battery-electric replacement costing $14.2 million. The national Clean Coasts Fund would pay 40% of the electric vessel's cost if the Council commits before 30 September.

## Council constraints

The Finance Committee will not raise the annual ferry subsidy above $1.8 million. Any decision to buy a new vessel needs evidence of sustained use and a funding plan before the March budget vote. Councillors from the harbour ward have said they will not back a plan that cuts winter service for islanders.

## Timeline and open questions

The grant deadline is 30 September. Grid upgrades for charging at both terminals are expected to take 14 to 18 months, and the diesel vessel would keep running throughout. Officers have not yet agreed how the cost of the charging upgrades would be shared. The operator has proposed a fare increase to cover part of that cost; the Council has not taken a position on the size of any increase.

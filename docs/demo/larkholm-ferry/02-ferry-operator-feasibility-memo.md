# Larkholm Ferry Company: electric ferry feasibility memo

This is a fictional memo written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## Ridership

Our ticketing system recorded 365,000 one-way crossings in 2025. About 58% of them were between June and September.

## What an electric ferry would change

A battery-electric vessel would cut fuel use and operating emissions on the route by about 90% and save roughly $0.9 million a year in fuel. Charging at both terminals needs grid upgrades that will take 14 to 18 months, so the diesel vessel must keep sailing until they are finished.

## Our proposal

To protect the battery and leave time for charging, we propose a reduced winter timetable of four daily return sailings from November to March, instead of today's six. We also propose a 12% fare increase to help pay for the charging equipment.

## Risks we want the Council to understand

Battery vessels have less power in reserve in heavy weather, so more winter sailings may be cancelled in storms. If the grant deadline is missed, the electric option is unlikely to be affordable. We do not yet know how many islanders will accept fewer winter sailings, and we have not surveyed them.

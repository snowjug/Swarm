# Stakeholder statements on the ferry proposal

This is a fictional collection of statements written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## Green Tide Alliance

The Alliance polled 400 visitors and residents and found that 74% support an electric ferry. The Alliance states that islanders overwhelmingly prefer the electric ferry even with fewer winter sailings. It estimates that the diesel ferry emits about 2,900 tonnes of carbon dioxide a year and urges the Council to accept the Clean Coasts Fund grant before 30 September. It proposes paying for winter service from summer fares.

## Harbour Business Council

Visitors account for about 70% of summer crossings, and summer visitor spending is worth around $9.5 million a year to island shops, guest houses and cafes. The Business Council supports an electric ferry because it would strengthen the island's green image. It warns that any fare increase above 5% would reduce day-trip bookings and asks that construction never disrupt the June to September season. It wants eight summer sailings a day.

# Scenario assumptions (analyst-provided)

This is a fictional set of planning assumptions written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. They are inputs chosen by the analyst, not facts from any source.

- A1. The Council commits to the electric ferry before the grant deadline, so the Clean Coasts Fund pays 40% of the vessel cost.
- A2. Fares rise by 12% when the electric ferry enters service.
- A3. From November to March the timetable is cut from six to four daily return sailings.
- A4. Charging works finish about 16 months after the decision, and the diesel vessel keeps sailing until then.
- A5. No new subsidy is available beyond the current $1.8 million ceiling.
- A6. Stakeholders learn about decisions through a broadcast notice board that everyone sees and through smaller topic communities where like-minded people talk.

These assumptions frame the what-if question. They are not predictions, and the simulation does not test whether they are realistic.

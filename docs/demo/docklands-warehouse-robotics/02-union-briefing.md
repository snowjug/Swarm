# Docklands Warehouse Workers' Union: briefing on robotics

This is a fictional union briefing written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## Concerns

The Union estimates that about 300 of the 1,400 roles on site are at risk once the robots are installed, and that supervision and maintenance posts will be far fewer than the roles they replace. It asks for a guaranteed retraining offer and a no-compulsory-redundancy agreement before any rollout.

## What the Union accepts

The Union does not oppose automation of the heaviest lifting tasks and would back a pilot with safety monitoring.

# Docklands Logistics: warehouse robotics proposal from the vendor

This is a fictional vendor proposal written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## Claims

The vendor says its robots would raise throughput by 35% at full rollout and that no roles would be lost, because staff would move to supervision and maintenance. The equipment would cost $9 million, with installation over 18 months.

## Conditions

The vendor assumes the warehouse floor can be resurfaced during the quarterly shutdown and that order volumes stay at current levels.

# Regional Health Board: clinic audit note

This is a fictional audit note written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## Findings

The median wait for a routine appointment in 2025 was 26 days at Hartwell Community Clinic. The no-show rate in 2025 was 11% of booked appointments.

## Concerns

The Board is concerned that same-day booking could disadvantage patients who cannot use the telephone at 8:00. The Board has not reviewed the clinic's proposal.

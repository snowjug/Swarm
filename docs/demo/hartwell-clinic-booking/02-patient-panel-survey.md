# Hartwell Patients' Panel: booking survey summary

This is a fictional survey summary written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## Who answered

The Panel surveyed 640 registered patients and received replies from 47% of them.

## What patients said

63% said a same-day option would help them. 29% worry about long telephone queues at 8:00. 71% of patients aged 65 and over prefer to book ahead.

## What the Panel asks

The Panel asks the clinic to keep at least 60% of appointments bookable in advance and to offer online booking.

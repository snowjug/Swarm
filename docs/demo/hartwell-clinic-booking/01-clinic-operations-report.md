# Hartwell Community Clinic: booking review

This is a fictional operations report written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## Waiting times

The median wait for a routine appointment in 2025 was 19 days. Patients who ask for an urgent appointment are seen the same day.

## Missed appointments

The no-show rate in 2025 was 8% of booked appointments.

## Proposal

The clinic proposes releasing 40% of daily appointments for same-day booking from 1 July, with the remainder booked in advance. Telephone lines would open at 8:00 each morning.

# Eastfield University Estates: parking proposal

This is a fictional proposal written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## Today

Peak weekday occupancy of the car parks was 92% in 2025. About 400 spaces are free to staff and students.

## Proposal

Estates proposes replacing the free spaces with 250 paid permits from September 2026. Each permit would cost $320 a year. A campus shuttle would run every 12 minutes between the station and the main gate.

## Why

Estates says the car parks are full at peak times and that the revenue would pay for the shuttle and for safer cycle storage.

# Eastfield Staff Association: parking briefing

This is a fictional briefing written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## What members counted

Peak weekday occupancy of the car parks was 78% in 2025, according to the Association's own counts on twelve mornings. The Association was told that each permit would cost $450 a year.

## What members said

The Association surveyed 520 staff and received replies from 61% of them. 58% oppose paid parking, and many ask for a lower charge for part-time staff.

# Eastfield Student Union: parking survey summary

This is a fictional survey summary written for a scenario-simulation demonstration. All names, places and figures are invented.

## Who answered

The Union surveyed 1,900 students and received replies from 38% of them.

## What students said

77% oppose paid parking. 54% of respondents commute by car at least twice a week. 22% said they would use a shuttle every 12 minutes.

## What the Union asks

The Union asks the University to exempt students on low incomes from any permit charge and to keep the shuttle free.
